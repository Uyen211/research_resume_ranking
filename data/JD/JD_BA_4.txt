About the Job
JOB DESCRIPTION

Control reports, analyze and evaluate the business performance of assigned departments. Organize the development, operation, and automation of reports.

Provide business data and support departments in resolving issues related to systems, accounting, and data organization.
Collect and manage data.
Analysis: Conduct analysis of business performance results of business departments, identify factors affecting business performance, and quantify the impact of these factors. Develop a reporting system for analysis, from detailed unit levels to overall departmental levels, promptly informing about newly emerging issues identified through financial data and updating relevant changes. Develop and implement short- and medium-term forecasts based on data monitoring and analysis.

Reporting: Lead the development, implementation, and quality control of financial and management information and management reporting systems for business units. Organize and participate in the development of processes and policies to improve the efficiency and transparency of financial and management reports.

Consulting: Proactively advise business units on issues related to product and service performance evaluation, customer performance evaluation, based on data analysis and insights. Advise on issues related to recording, accounting, and evaluation of business operations, including those not yet covered by specific regulations.

Optimization: Develop and apply statistical models and data mining techniques (such as SQL, programming languages, R, Python) to automate operations, and integrate big data and mathematical applications to improve the quality of analysis and the efficiency of professional work processes.
Business Planning: - Participate in supporting the development of business unit budgets, from overall to detailed plans. - Provide information and calculate financial indicators. - Monitor the budget implementation of business units, promptly alerting to any signs or issues affecting the implementation of the units' plans. - Support business units in building a system of annual performance indicators (KPIs).

Partner Management: Coordinate with middle management of business units and supporting units.

JOB REQUIREMENTS

Bachelor's degree or higher in economics / finance / banking / accounting / auditing

Minimum 7 years of experience in financial analysis, business operations management, or management accounting in financial institutions, banks, or credit organizations
Understanding of banking and financial accounting operations, asset and liability management, and management accounting
Good understanding of basic financial models or data, tools, and reporting techniques, and the ability to apply them Diverse product/field analysis and reporting
Knowledge of relevant laws and regulations, expertise in financial analysis, and business models of financial institutions.
Proficient in handling big data/analytical reporting
Proficient in data analysis software and Microsoft Office tools (especially SQL, Excel, PowerPoint, Power BI, Office 365)
Good English skills
Systematic thinking; strong critical, creative, analytical, and synthesis skills; ability to independently research and apply technology to work.

Excellent communication, negotiation, and persuasion skills.
