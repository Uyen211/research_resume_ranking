About the job
Company Overview



Xoserve is the UK gas industry’s Central Data Services Provider, playing a critical role in supporting the safe, efficient and reliable operation of Britain’s gas market. It provides a single, trusted source of industry data and services for gas suppliers, shippers and transporters, ensuring information is accurate, secure, and consistently shared across the sector. By managing the central gas register and providing insight on gas flows across the network, Xoserve helps the industry balance supply and demand and supports effective market processes that benefit consumers. As the energy system transitions, Xoserve is committed to enabling affordable, accessible decarbonisation by supporting the integration of green gas and biomethane into the UK’s energy mix. Xoserve is an equal employment opportunity employer, and we are building a diverse, inclusive, and authentic workplace for everyone.


Role Summary



Reporting to the OPTiX Project Director (Organisation, Process & Technology in Xoserve), the Business Analyst will contribute to a broad range of initiatives across Xoserve’s change portfolios. The role focuses on eliciting, analysing and documenting current and future state processes and requirements to inform effective solution selection and delivery, ensuring alignment with strategic objectives. 



This position is suited to an experienced and motivated Business Analyst looking to make a meaningful impact within Xoserve’s transformation agenda. 



Responsibilities

 
Elicit, document, prioritise, validate, and communicate business, functional, and non-functional requirements, user stories and related acceptance criteria across projects and programmes
Maintain end to end traceability from business objectives and problem statements through to requirements, solution design, testing and delivered outcomes
Support stakeholders in shaping business needs and future-state designs, identifying risks, constraints & dependencies
Lead discovery activities including current-/target-state analysis, feasibility assessment
Work collaboratively with Solution Architects to support technology options evaluation
Create process maps, use case diagrams, data flow diagrams, functional specifications, and other Business Analysis artefacts
Ensure requirements and analysis outputs meet agreed standards and quality expectations
Facilitate workshops for requirements gathering, problem-solving, and solution design
Perform gap analysis and support business case development
Act as liaison between business teams, delivery teams, and external suppliers
Provide analysis expertise throughout the full project lifecycle, ensuring solutions meet business objectives
Promote best practice, continuous improvement, and adherence to BA methodologies
