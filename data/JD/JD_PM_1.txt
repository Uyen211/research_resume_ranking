About the Job
JOB DESCRIPTION:

Planning project implementation, obtaining investment approvals, negotiating and signing contracts with contractors and partners.
Working with relevant authorities to obtain project implementation permits, etc.
Participating in working groups within the alliance, managing projects, and ensuring timely implementation.
Participating in domestic and international telecommunications events, industry conferences, and work programs within submarine cable alliances.
Perform other tasks as assigned.
REQUIREMENTS:

Education: Bachelor's degree or higher (Graduates from Foreign Trade University, National Economics University, or other universities…)
Experience: At least 3 years in a similar position
Preference will be given to candidates with experience in the telecommunications or technology industry
Fluent English (Chinese language skills are a plus)
Strong negotiation and communication skills
Ability to plan, allocate resources, monitor progress, manage risks, organize and supervise project implementation.
Problem-solving: Analytical thinking, ability to handle unforeseen situations.
Communication & Teamwork: Communication, presentation, and team building skills. Independent and proactive work ethic, teamwork skills, ability to handle high work pressure.
Candidates with professional project management certifications are an advantage.
