About the Job
JOB DETAILS
System Administration: Responsible for managing, operating, and updating content on the FPT.vn website's CMS system.
Display & Content Management: Comprehensively manage the product system, display space (Banners, Images, Popups), and set up the Voucher/Promotion system on the website.
Product Development & UX/UI: Research, propose, and collaborate with the team (Design, Dev) to develop new features and improve the user experience (User Flow) on the website.
Landing Page Management: Participate in building, managing, optimizing, and continuously upgrading the Landing Page system to support Marketing/Sales campaigns.
Measurement & Evaluation: Monitor data, evaluate, and measure the effectiveness (conversion rate, interaction, etc.) of new features after launch.
Quality Control (Technical/Audit): Regularly review technical issues of the website (loading speed, logical errors, display bugs) and propose timely optimization solutions.

JOB REQUIREMENTS
Education equivalent to a university degree in Marketing, E-commerce, Information Technology, etc.
Work experience: 3 years of experience in Product Executive or E-commerce Operation positions at large companies.

- Understanding of UX/UI and user flow.

- Experience in creating Customer Journey Maps and writing feature requirements documents.

- Proficient in using data analytics tools (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer...).

Mindset: Strong logical thinking, data-driven, customer-centric mindset.

Soft skills: Excellent time management, problem-solving, and teamwork skills (especially when working across with IT/Dev and Marketing teams).
