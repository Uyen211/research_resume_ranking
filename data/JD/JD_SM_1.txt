Job Description
Initiate and plan project implementation (scope, timeline, milestone, release).
Collaborate with PO/Tech Lead to build EPIC, roadmap, and work structure in Jira.

Develop a Work Breakdown Statement (WBS), organize and control the team's execution progress.
Monitor, evaluate, and adjust the plan to ensure deadlines and quality.
Establish a dashboard to track progress, workload, and risk alerts.
Coordinate Scrum Events (Daily, Planning, Refinement, Nexus).
Manage resources (internal and outsourced), forecast resource shortages/surpluses.
Proactively identify and address risks and changes during implementation.
Work with clients/stakeholders to agree on project requirements and objectives.
Provide regular/ad hoc reports on project status; organize acceptance testing and handover.
