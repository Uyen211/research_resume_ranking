Role: Data Engineer - FPT Software

Location: HCM, HN

Working model: Hybrid/Remote


Responsibilities

Design, develop, and maintain scalable data pipelines and ETL processes.
Optimize and manage data storage solutions, including databases and data warehouses.
Collaborate with data scientists and analysts to understand data requirements and deliver high-quality data solutions.
Implement data quality and validation processes to ensure data accuracy and integrity.
Monitor and troubleshoot data systems to ensure optimal performance and reliability.
Stay up-to-date with the latest industry trends and technologies in data engineering.
Mentor and provide guidance to junior data engineers.


Qualifications

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
5+ years of experience in data engineering or a related role.
Strong proficiency in SQL and experience with relational databases (e.g., MySQL, PostgreSQL).
Experience with big data technologies (e.g., Hadoop, Spark, Kafka).
Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, Java, or Scala.
Experience with cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and their data services.
Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
Efficient English communication skills (both verbal and written).
Preferred Qualifications:

Experience with data modeling and data architecture design.
Knowledge of data governance and security best practices.
Familiarity with machine learning and data science concepts.
